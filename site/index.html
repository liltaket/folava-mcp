Data and access

What Folava can read, where your credentials live and who sees your course material.

Data handling

Reviewed reads

Folava uses reviewed Canvas GET requests. Uploads, submissions and options that mark content as read are blocked. Canvas may still record access or refresh progress data.

Credentials stay local

Keep credentials outside your chat, in your OS keyring or a protected secret source.

Your host’s terms apply

Your MCP host may send course material to its model provider. That provider’s data terms apply.

Development preview

Folava is an independent development preview. Instructure approval for this MCP integration has not been obtained. Deployment limits.

Access depends on your account permissions and institution. External timetables, locked content and third-party tools may be unavailable. Check the originals in Canvas.
