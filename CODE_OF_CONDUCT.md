# Community expectations

Be considerate, specific and constructive. Critique the change, not the person.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or publish private information about
other participants. Keep student records and credentials out of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to protect contributors. To report a concern, contact a maintainer
through an available private GitHub channel. If none is available, request a private
channel in a minimal issue without naming affected people or disclosing details.
