Why I made this

Hi, I’m Bruno. To be honest, I find the Canvas interface confusing. I kept clicking around just to work out what was coming up, when what I really wanted was to ask my agents for help, or simply ask ChatGPT what’s due this week.

So I built Folava. It’s careful on purpose: it only reads, it runs on your own computer and your Canvas token never goes into the chat. It’s a side project and still early, so if something breaks or your school’s Canvas does something odd, open an issue and I’ll take a look.

What it can and can’t do

It only reads. Every request is a reviewed Canvas GET. Uploads, submissions and options that mark content as read are blocked. Canvas may still record that you looked, or refresh progress data.

Your token stays out of the chat. Keep it in your OS keyring or another protected secret source.

Your agent’s provider sees what it reads. Your MCP host may send course material to its model provider, and that provider’s data terms apply. Data handling

Still a preview

Folava is an independent project, and Instructure hasn’t approved this MCP integration. Deployment limits

What you can see depends on your account and your school. External timetables, locked content and third-party tools may be missing, so check the originals in Canvas.
