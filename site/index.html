The resource client only sends reviewed GET requests. It rejects uploads, submissions, arbitrary destinations and options that mark content as read.

Credentials are configured outside the conversation and kept in your OS keyring or a protected secret source. Folava has no telemetry or model SDK.

Your MCP host may share selected course material with its model provider. Choose a host appropriate for your institution and your data.

Know what you’re installing

This is an independent development preview. Access depends on your account permissions, OAuth scopes and institution. Instructure’s current policy explicitly addresses MCP approval; that approval has not been obtained for this project. Read the deployment limits.

Canvas remains the source of truth. External timetables, locked content and third-party LTI tools may not be accessible.
