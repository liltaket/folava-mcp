Data and access

Folava uses reviewed Canvas GET requests. Uploads, submissions and options that mark content as read are blocked. Canvas may still record access or refresh progress data.

Keep credentials outside your chat, in your OS keyring or a protected secret source.

Your MCP host may send course material to its model provider. That provider’s data terms apply.
